Prices and VAT

How much does colour PPF cost?

Is a detail included with PPF?

Yes. Frontal, full and colour PPF each come with a complimentary detail.

Paint protection film

What is PPF?

Paint protection film is a clear (or coloured) TPU film fitted over the paint. It takes the stone chips, light scratches and road grime that would otherwise mark the paint.

Clear, matte or colour PPF?

Clear keeps the car looking as it left the factory. Matte gives gloss paint a soft matte look. Colour PPF changes the colour of the car and still covers the paint.

What does frontal PPF cover?

The complete front bumper (painted sections included), the entire bonnet, both front fenders and both painted side-mirror caps.

Is there a warranty on PPF?

Yes. PPF comes with a 5-year warranty.

Does PPF stop every chip?

No. PPF is not armour: a big enough hit can still damage the film and the paint under it. Clear film also shows what's underneath, so existing chips stay visible.

Colour PPF

Is colour PPF the same as a vinyl wrap?

No. Vinyl wraps are usually PVC film. Colour PPF is TPU, the same kind of film as clear PPF, so it protects the paint while it changes the colour.

How many colours can I choose from?

There are 394 films in our supplier's range, in gloss, satin, matte, metallic, pearl and colour-shift, chrome, carbon and holographic finishes. Colours on screen are a guide, so ask to see the real film before you choose.

Are the door shuts included?

Yes. Our colour PPF includes the door shuts.

Detailing, polishing and ceramic coating

What products do you use?

Our main detailing range is Detail Ease, with alternatives such as Meguiar's when the car needs them. For ceramic coating we use Detail Ease or Meguiar's.

Do you do interior and exterior details?

Yes, both. A full detail covers the outside and inside; an interior or exterior detail on its own is quoted per car.

Do you detail engine bays?

Yes, as an add-on. We inspect the engine bay first, before we take on the work.

How long does a ceramic coating last?

It depends on the package and on how the car is looked after: 6 to 12 months for Essential Gloss, up to 2 years for Signature Ceramic and 3 to 5 years for Premium Ceramic.

Can you put a ceramic coating over PPF?

Yes, over gloss PPF. Over matte PPF, a coating would take away the matte effect.

Is PPF better than ceramic coating?

They do different jobs. PPF is a film that takes stone chips. A ceramic coating is a thin layer that adds gloss and makes washing easier, but it won't stop chips.
