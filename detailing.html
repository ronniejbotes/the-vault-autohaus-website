Interior detailing

The inside of a car collects more than you'd think. Dust settles in the vents, crumbs work into the seat rails, and spills soak into the carpet underlay. Pet hair weaves itself into fabric and needs more than a vacuum.

Good interior detailing deals with what you touch and breathe every day. Fabric is shampooed and the dirty water extracted, so stains and smells come out instead of being masked. Leather is cleaned gently, then conditioned.

Detailing or a car wash?

A car wash gets the loose dirt off. A detail goes after what a wash leaves behind: grime in the door shuts, dust in the vents, crumbs in the seat rails.

The method matters too. Rushed washing with dirty mitts and brushes is a common cause of swirl marks. A detail takes the time to clean the paint without scratching it.

A detail isn't a polish, though. Detailing cleans the paint. Machine polishing removes swirls and light scratches, and it's quoted per car.

When to book a detail

  • Before you sell, so the car shows well in photos and at viewings.
  • After you buy a used car, to start fresh inside.
  • After a road trip, a beach holiday or a dusty farm visit.
  • When kids, pets or spilt coffee have taken over the interior.
  • Before a wedding, a matric dance or a car show.
