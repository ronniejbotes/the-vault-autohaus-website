Interior detailing

The inside of a car collects more than you'd think. Dust settles in the vents, crumbs work into the seat rails, and spills soak into the carpet underlay. Pet hair weaves itself into fabric and needs more than a vacuum.

Every full detail deals with what you touch and breathe every day. The carpets, seats and boot are vacuumed, the vents and crevices detailed, fabric seats spot-cleaned and leather cleaned and conditioned. A deodorising treatment finishes the job.

For deeper stains and smells, add carpet and upholstery deep extraction. Heavy pet hair has its own add-on.

Detailing or a car wash?

A car wash gets the loose dirt off. A detail goes after what a wash leaves behind: grime in the door shuts, dust in the vents, crumbs in the seat rails.

The method matters too. Rushed washing with dirty mitts and brushes is a common cause of swirl marks. A detail takes the time to clean the paint without scratching it.

A detail isn't a polish, though. Detailing cleans the paint. Machine polishing removes swirls and light scratches. It's an optional add-on, from R1 200 for a basic gloss polish.

When to book a detail

What's the difference between a valet and a detail?

A valet is a quick clean, inside and out. A full detail is slower and goes deeper, with a clay bar treatment for the paint, cleaned door jambs and boot shuts, and detailed vents and crevices.

Can you get rid of stains, pet hair and smells?

Every full detail includes spot-cleaned fabric seats and a deodorising treatment. For more, add carpet and upholstery deep extraction or pet hair removal. Many stains and smells can be cleaned out or reduced, but some old stains are permanent. We'll tell you what to expect when we inspect the car.
