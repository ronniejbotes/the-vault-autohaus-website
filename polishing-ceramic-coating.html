What machine polishing does

Most modern car paint has a clear coat on top. Swirls, light scratches and water spots sit in that clear coat. A machine polisher levels it very slightly, until the marks are gone or much harder to see.

A one-step polish lifts the gloss and removes lighter marks. Heavier correction uses a cutting compound first, then a finishing polish. We offer four levels:

  • Basic gloss polish: from R1 200
  • Light correction: from R2 000
  • Medium correction: from R3 500
  • Heavy correction: we need to inspect the car first

There are limits. Run a fingernail across a scratch: if it catches, it's probably too deep to polish out fully. Stone chips need touch-up paint, not polish. Each polish also removes a little clear coat, so it isn't something to repeat often.

Polishing suits a car that looks dull, shows swirls in the sun, or is about to be sold.

What a ceramic coating does, and what it doesn't

A ceramic coating is a liquid applied to clean paint, where it bonds and hardens into a thin, glossy layer. Water beads and runs off, and dirt sticks less, so washing is quicker. It also helps protect the paint from bird droppings, tree sap and sun.

It isn't armour, though. A coating won't stop stone chips or deep scratches, and the car still needs regular washing. For chips, you need PPF.

How long a coating lasts depends on the product and how the car is washed. We use Detail Ease or Meguiar's, and each package below says how long it lasts.

A coating suits a new car, a freshly polished one, or any car you'd like to wash in less time.

Why the paint is polished before it's coated

A coating locks in whatever is under it, swirls included. So the paint is washed, decontaminated and polished first. Then the polishing oils are wiped off so the coating can bond properly.

After coating, the car needs time to cure. [Cure time and first-wash advice for the coating used] After that, a gentle hand wash with a pH-neutral shampoo helps the coating last.

Ceramic coating, wax or PPF?

Wax and spray sealants are cheap and easy, but they wear off within weeks or months. A ceramic coating lasts much longer, with less upkeep. PPF is a physical film, so it's the only one of the three that takes stone chips.

They also work together. A common setup is PPF on the front, where the chips happen, and a ceramic coating on the rest. We can also coat over gloss PPF. Over matte PPF, a coating would take away the matte effect. Ask us to price the combination.

Polishing and ceramic coating questions

How much does ceramic coating cost in Germiston?

At The Vault, ceramic coating comes in three packages: Essential Gloss at R2 500 to R3 500, Signature Ceramic at R4 500 to R6 000 and Premium Ceramic at R6 500 to R8 500. We're not VAT registered, so no VAT is added. We inspect the car before booking and confirm the price then.

How much does machine polishing cost?

A basic gloss polish starts at R1 200, light correction at R2 000 and medium correction at R3 500. For heavy correction, we need to inspect the car first.

How long does a ceramic coating take?

One to three days. The car stays with us while the work is done.

Is ceramic coating worth it?

It is if you want lasting gloss and quicker washing, with less upkeep. If you mainly want protection from stone chips, PPF is the better buy.

How long does a ceramic coating last?

It depends on the package and on how the car is looked after: 6 to 12 months for Essential Gloss, up to 2 years for Signature Ceramic and 3 to 5 years for Premium Ceramic.

Which ceramic coating do you use?

We use Detail Ease or Meguiar's.

Can polishing remove scratches?

It removes swirls and light scratches in the clear coat. If your fingernail catches in a scratch, it's probably too deep to remove fully, but polishing can make it less visible.

Does a ceramic coating stop stone chips?

No. A coating is far too thin to stop a stone. For chips, fit PPF on the areas that take the hits.

Can you put a ceramic coating on PPF?

Yes, we can coat over gloss PPF, which makes it easier to clean. Over matte PPF, a coating would take away the matte effect. WhatsApp us for a price.
