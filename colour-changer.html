Before you choose a colour

A phone or laptop screen can't show metallic flake, a pearl shift or a chrome mirror the way daylight does. Use the colour changer to narrow down your choice, then ask to see the real film.

Changing the whole car's colour starts at R40 000. That covers the door shuts, a complimentary detail and a 5-year warranty. Large SUVs can cost up to R5 000 more. No VAT is added, because we aren't VAT registered, and the final price is confirmed when we inspect the car.

If the film you pick has to be ordered in, it takes 2 to 3 weeks to arrive. Fitting it then takes about 4 to 5 working days.

How to use the colour changer

  1. Choose a finish: gloss, satin, matte, metallic, pearl and shift, chrome, carbon or holographic.
  2. Narrow it down by colour, or search by name, like Miami or emerald.
  3. Tap a circle to put that film on the car.
  4. Tap the red button to send us the colour on WhatsApp, with your car's make, model and year.
