Paint protection film (PPF) in Germiston and Bedfordview

Paint protection film (PPF) is a clear or matte film fitted over your car's paint. It takes the stone chips, light scratches and road grime that would otherwise mark the paint. What PPF does, and what it doesn't

PPF, also called clear bra, is a thermoplastic polyurethane (TPU) film. It's tough and flexible, so it stretches over curves and soaks up small stone strikes. The clear film we fit is [Clear PPF brand].

The film sits between your paint and the road. Stone chips, sand, bug splatter and bird droppings hit the film instead of the paint. If a piece of film is damaged, that panel can be re-filmed, usually without touching the paint.

PPF is not armour. A big enough hit can still damage the film and the paint under it. Clear film also shows what's underneath, so existing chips stay visible. Swirl marks are best polished out before the film goes on.

Clear or matte PPF

Clear PPF protects the paint without changing its colour. The colour and gloss of the paint show through the film.

Matte PPF protects the paint and changes the finish. Over gloss paint, it gives a soft, flat look without a respray. Take the film off later and the gloss paint is still underneath. Matte film also suits factory matte paint, where gloss film would add shine.

On a gloss car, the matte look only works if every painted panel is covered. Want a new colour instead? See colour PPF and browse the 394 colours.

Frontal or full PPF

Frontal PPF covers the front of the car, where stone chips hit hardest. Ours covers:

  • the complete front bumper, including painted sections
  • the entire bonnet, not just the leading edge
  • both front fenders
  • both painted side-mirror caps

Frontal suits a daily driver that does a lot of highway or gravel kilometres. It protects the parts that chip first for less than half the price of full PPF.

Full PPF covers the whole painted body. It suits new cars, cars you plan to keep for years, and cars where original paint matters at resale. It also covers the lower doors and sills, which catch grit thrown up by the tyres.

Is PPF worth it?

It is if stone chips bother you, you drive long distances, or you want the original paint intact when you sell. If budget is tight, frontal PPF covers the areas that chip most.

How long does PPF last?

It depends on the film, how much the car is driven and how it's cared for. [Clear PPF brand] is rated for [Film lifespan], and the job carries [Warranty].

Is PPF better than ceramic coating?

They do different jobs. PPF is a film that takes stone chips. A ceramic coating is a thin layer that adds gloss and makes washing easier, but it won't stop chips.

Can you put PPF on a used car?

Yes, but clear film shows what's underneath. Chips stay visible, and swirl marks are best polished out first. Polishing starts at R1 200. When we inspect the car, we'll tell you whether it needs a polish before the film goes on.
