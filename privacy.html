Privacy policy

What personal information The Vault SA collects through this website, WhatsApp and calls, why, who else sees it, and your rights under South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA). Effective 29 October 2026.

Who we are

The Vault SA is the responsible party for the personal information described here. We work in Germiston and Bedfordview, Gauteng, by appointment only. For anything on this page, WhatsApp or call us on 084 407 6980.

What we collect and why

  • WhatsApp messages and calls. Your name, phone number and whatever you tell us about your car and the job, including any photos you send. This website has no forms: the WhatsApp buttons open WhatsApp on your own device, and nothing reaches us until you send the message there.
  • How visitors use the site. We use Microsoft Clarity to see how visitors use this website (where people click and scroll, and anonymised recordings of visits) so we can make it easier to use. Clarity sets cookies and hides sensitive details typed into fields. Microsoft processes this data under its privacy statement.

We use your details to reply to you, give you a quote, book your car in, do the work and keep a record of it. Giving us your details is voluntary, but without a phone number we cannot get back to you.

We do not sell personal information, we do not pass it to anyone for their marketing, and we will not send you marketing you did not ask for.

Who else receives it

  • WhatsApp, run by Meta, carries the messages you send us, under its own privacy policy.
  • Hostinger hosts this website. Like any web host, its servers see the requests your browser makes for each page.
  • Microsoft runs Clarity, as described above.
  • Google supplies the fonts this website uses, and jsDelivr supplies some of its code, so both receive your device's internet (IP) address and browser details when a page opens.

The Instagram, TikTok and Facebook links open those sites only if you click them. Meta, Microsoft, Google, jsDelivr and Hostinger may store information outside South Africa, for example in the United States, under their own privacy terms.

Cookies and how to block them

Cookies are small files a website saves in your browser. This website sets none of its own. Microsoft Clarity sets _clck (up to a year) and _clsk (one day) to join the pages of a visit together, and Microsoft may set cookies of its own. To block or delete cookies, use your browser's settings or browse in a private window. Everything on this website still works with cookies blocked.

How long we keep information

  • Enquiries and records of work: we keep them for as long as we need them to respond, to do the work, and to meet our legal and accounting obligations.
  • Clarity data: kept by Microsoft for the period its privacy statement sets out.

Your rights under POPIA

You can ask us:

  • whether we hold personal information about you, and what it is;
  • to correct or delete it;
  • to stop using it, by objecting; and
  • to stop using it where you gave consent and now withdraw it.

WhatsApp or call 084 407 6980. We will reply within a reasonable time, as POPIA requires. If you are not happy with our answer, you can complain to the Information Regulator of South Africa at inforegulator.org.za.

Keeping it safe, and changes

This website uses a secure (HTTPS) connection and stores nothing you type. If we change how we handle personal information, we will update this page and the date at the top.
