What we do

Protect it. Change it. Finish it.

Four kinds of work: film that guards the paint, film that changes the colour, parts that change the shape, and the detailing that makes it all look right.

Protect

Paint protection film

PPF is a tough, clear layer of TPU (thermoplastic polyurethane) that sits on top of your paint, so stone chips, scratches and road grime hit the film instead. Go clear to keep the factory look, or matte to turn gloss paint into a soft matte finish.

  • Clear PPF
  • Matte PPF
  • Frontal or full body
  • 5-year warranty

Change

Colour PPF

Change the colour of the whole car and protect the paint in the same job. Colour PPF is TPU film, like clear PPF, so the car gets a new colour and the paint underneath stays covered. Door shuts are included. There are 394 films to choose from, in gloss, satin, matte, metallic, chrome and carbon finishes.

Questions

Good to know

What is PPF?

Paint protection film is a clear (or coloured) TPU film fitted over the paint. It takes the stone chips, light scratches and road grime that would otherwise mark the paint.

Clear, matte or colour PPF?

Clear keeps the car looking exactly as it left the factory. Matte gives gloss paint a soft matte look. Colour PPF changes the colour of the car, and still covers the paint.

Is colour PPF the same as a vinyl wrap?

No. Vinyl wraps are usually PVC film. Colour PPF is TPU, the same kind of film as clear PPF, so it protects the paint while it changes the colour.

How much does PPF cost?

Frontal PPF is R15 000, full PPF is R35 000 and colour PPF is R40 000. Each one includes a complimentary detail. Matte PPF costs the same as clear. Large SUVs can cost up to R3 000 extra for clear PPF and up to R5 000 extra for colour PPF. We're not VAT registered, so no VAT is added.

Is there a warranty on PPF?

Yes. PPF comes with a 5-year warranty.

How long does a job take?

Clear PPF, colour PPF and a full detail take 4 to 5 working days. A ceramic coating takes 1 to 3 days. If your colour PPF has to be ordered in, allow 2 to 3 weeks for it to arrive.

Can I get a detail without PPF?

Yes. A detail on its own is R4 000 to R6 000, depending on the car. Polishing starts at R1 200, ceramic coating packages at R2 500, and headlight and tail light restoration is R2 000.

Do you need to see the car before booking?

Yes. We inspect every car before we book the work. That way we can give you a professional recommendation and confirm the price.

Can you fit a wing or other exterior parts?

Yes. We fit rear wings and other exterior parts, and quote for the parts and your car. Send us the part or the look you want on WhatsApp.
