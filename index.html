What we do

Protect it. Change it. Finish it.

Four kinds of work: film that guards the paint, film that changes the colour, parts that change the shape, and the detailing that makes it all look right.

Protect

Paint protection film

PPF is a tough, clear layer of TPU (thermoplastic polyurethane) that sits on top of your paint, so stone chips, scratches and road grime hit the film instead. Go clear to keep the factory look, or matte to turn gloss paint into a soft matte finish.

Change

Colour PPF

Change the colour of the whole car and protect the paint in the same job. Colour PPF is TPU film, like clear PPF, so the car gets a new colour and the paint underneath stays covered. There are 394 films to choose from, in gloss, satin, matte, metallic, chrome and carbon finishes.

Questions

Good to know

What is PPF?

Paint protection film is a clear (or coloured) TPU film fitted over the paint. It takes the stone chips, light scratches and road grime that would otherwise mark the paint.

Clear, matte or colour?

Clear keeps the car looking exactly as it left the factory. Matte gives gloss paint a soft matte look. Colour PPF changes the colour of the car, and still covers the paint.

Is colour PPF the same as a vinyl wrap?

No. Vinyl wraps are usually PVC film. Colour PPF is TPU, the same kind of film as clear PPF, so it protects the paint while it changes the colour.

How much does PPF cost?

Frontal PPF is R15 000, full PPF is R35 000 and colour PPF is R40 000. Each one includes a complimentary detail.

Can I get a detail without PPF?

Yes. A detail on its own is R4 000 to R6 000, depending on the car. Polishing and ceramic coating are quoted per car.
