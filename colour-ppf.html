What colour PPF is

Colour PPF is thermoplastic polyurethane (TPU), the same kind of film as clear PPF. Clear film protects the paint and lets it show through. Colour film protects the paint and covers it with a new colour.

Think of it as a colour change wrap, done in protective film instead of vinyl. Your original paint stays under the film the whole time. If you change your mind, or want to sell in the factory colour, the film can come off. Tell us about any resprays first, because removal is riskier on aftermarket paint.

Colour PPF or vinyl wrap?

Both change your car's colour, and both can come off later. The difference is the material and what it's made to do.

Vinyl suits a short-term look or a tighter budget. Colour PPF suits owners who want the new colour and the protection in one job.

How a colour change works

  1. Pick a colour in the colour changer and send it to us on WhatsApp, with your car's make, model and year.
  2. We inspect the car before booking, so we can give you a professional recommendation. Then we confirm the price and book a date. If your colour has to be ordered in, allow 2 to 3 weeks for it to arrive.
  3. You drop the car at the studio. It's washed and prepped, then the film goes on panel by panel. Door shuts are included.
  4. You collect the car in its new colour, with a complimentary detail. The work takes about 4 to 5 working days.

Care is the same as for clear PPF: hand wash, keep pressure washers off the edges and skip abrasive polishes. Adding a rear wing too? We also fit wings, spoilers and body parts.

Colour PPF questions

How much does colour PPF cost?

A full colour change in colour PPF starts at R40 000 at The Vault in Germiston and Bedfordview, door shuts included. That includes a complimentary detail and a 5-year warranty. Large SUVs can cost up to R5 000 extra. We're not VAT registered, so no VAT is added. We inspect the car before booking and confirm the price then.

Is colour PPF better than a vinyl wrap?

It depends on what you want. Colour PPF usually costs more, but it protects the paint as well as changing the colour. Vinyl is mainly about the look.

Can I see the colour before I decide?

Yes. Try all 394 films in the colour changer, then ask to see the real film. Screens don't show metallic, pearl and chrome finishes the way daylight does.

Can colour PPF be removed?

Yes, PPF is made to come off. Factory paint in good condition is the safest base, so tell us about any resprays before we start.

Do I need to tell my insurer about a colour change?

It's wise to, since your insurer should know the car's current colour. Ask your licensing office whether your registration papers need the new colour too.

How long does a colour change take?

The work takes about 4 to 5 working days, and the car stays at the studio while it's done. If your colour has to be ordered in, allow 2 to 3 weeks for it to arrive.

Are the door shuts included?

Yes. Our colour PPF includes the door shuts.
