All prices are starting prices in South African rand. We're not VAT registered, so no VAT is added. Large SUVs can cost up to R3 000 more for clear PPF and up to R5 000 more for colour PPF. Every PPF job, frontal, full or colour, comes with a complimentary detail.

Paint protection film (PPF)

PPF is a tough, clear layer of TPU (thermoplastic polyurethane) over your paint, so stone chips, scratches and road grime hit the film instead. Choose clear to keep the factory look, or matte to turn gloss paint into a soft matte finish.

Frontal PPF covers

  • The complete front bumper, painted sections included
  • The entire bonnet, not just the leading edge
  • Both front fenders
  • Both painted side-mirror caps

Colour PPF

Change the colour of the whole car and protect the paint in the same job. Colour PPF is TPU film, like clear PPF, so the car gets a new colour and the paint underneath stays covered.

Included

  • Door shuts
  • A complimentary detail
  • A 5-year warranty
  • 394 films to choose from: gloss, satin, matte, metallic, pearl and colour-shift, chrome, carbon and holographic

Price

Detailing

A full detail is a deep clean of your car's outside and inside, reaching the places a car wash misses. Our main product range is Detail Ease, with alternatives such as Meguiar's when the car needs them.

A full detail includes

  • Snow foam pre-wash and a full hand wash
  • Clay bar treatment for embedded contaminants
  • Wheels, arches, door jambs and boot shuts cleaned
  • Carpets, seats and boot vacuumed; vents and crevices detailed
  • Leather cleaned and conditioned, fabric seats spot-cleaned
  • An interior deodorising treatment

Polishing and ceramic coating

Machine polishing, also called paint correction, takes out swirl marks and light scratches and brings dull paint back to a gloss. A ceramic coating then helps keep that gloss and makes the car easier to wash. We use Detail Ease or Meguiar's.
